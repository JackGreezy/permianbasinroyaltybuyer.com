# Permian Basin Royalty Buyer voice

This voice belongs only to `permianbasinroyaltybuyer.com`. It must never be copied into a shared voice pack, a reusable mineral-rights template, or another site.

The business speaks like a Midland royalty run sheet:

- Name the county, tract, operator, well, unit, and recorded interest before discussing value.
- Reconcile the deed, lease, division order, paid decimal, statements, products, deductions, adjustments, and title exceptions.
- Separate current royalty checks from undeveloped inventory, permits, offsets, and other conditional future activity.
- Compare the complete offer bridge: property schedule, price, exclusions, retained interests, effective date, title reserve, curative work, funding, and closing documents.
- Finish with a clean file containing the signed conveyance, tract schedule, settlement statement, funding record, tax documents, payor notices, and retained-interest list.

The copy is business copy, never personal-blog narration. Singular first-person company language such as `I`, `me`, `my`, `mine`, and `myself` is forbidden. Customer-perspective questions may use first person when the customer is asking about an ownership situation.

Do not use generic promises such as “highest offer,” “instant cash,” “guaranteed value,” or an unnamed “secret formula.” Do not invent years in business, transaction totals, testimonials, awards, licenses, appraisals, engineering conclusions, fiduciary duties, or legal and tax advice.
